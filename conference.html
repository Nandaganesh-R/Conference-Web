The Conference

ACCTHPA 2025, the Seventh International Conference on "Advanced Computing and Communication Technologies for High Performance Applications (ACCTHPA)" is a prime conference which intends to provide a forum that brings together the researchers from academia and industry to convene and swap over the ideas and recent research work on the High-Performance Applications. Scheduled on July 18-19, 2025 at Federal Institute of Science and Technology (FISAT), Cochin, Kerala, India.

The theme for this year's conference is “Latest trends in High Performance Computing, Communication and Artificial Intelligence”. Driven by the immense knowledge gained in various fields of computing, there exists an incredible potential to develop HPC Applications in various fields of Science and Engineering that enable progress and betterment of humanity. While Machine learning systems emerge as a promising and futuristic technology of the 21st Century, the High-Performance Computation techniques have pushed the horizons of such technologies aiding in application development. We hope to see original contributions from interdisciplinary areas that make use of High-Performance computing.
