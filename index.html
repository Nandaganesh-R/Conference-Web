ABOUT

ICAIVES 2026 is an International Conference on AI, VLSI, Embedded Systems, Signal Processing and Communication. The conference brings together researchers, innovators and professionals to present research, discuss emerging challenges and foster collaboration.
More >

THEME

ICAIVES 2026 focuses on the convergence of Artificial Intelligence, VLSI, Embedded Systems, Signal Processing and Communication Technologies in shaping the future of intelligent systems and smart connectivity. The conference emphasizes theoretical advancements and real-world applications in automation, efficient computing, next-generation communication and sustainable electronics.

Call for Papers

ICAIVES 2026 invites researchers, innovators and professionals to present original research and cutting-edge solutions related to AI, VLSI, Embedded Systems, Signal Processing and Communication Technologies.
